Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Klartion is a self-hosted application. Your bank transactions, Notion data, and credentials never leave your machine — they are never sent to or stored on our servers. The only data we handle is what is strictly necessary to operate the licence system. This policy explains what that is.

Who we are

Klartion is an independent software product developed and maintained by David Alves, Portugal. You can reach us at support@david-alves.com.

What data we collect

We collect only the minimum necessary to operate the licence system:

  • Your email address — collected by Stripe when you purchase a licence, and stored on our licence server to deliver your licence key and provide support.
  • Licence activation records — when you activate your licence key, we store an anonymised machine fingerprint (a hash of your hostname) and the number of active activations. We do not store device names, IP addresses, or any personally identifiable hardware information.
We do not collect, store, or have access to your bank account data, transaction history, Notion content, Enable Banking or SimpleFIN credentials, SMTP passwords, or any financial information. All of this stays on your machine.

What we do not collect

  • Bank transactions or account balances
  • Notion database content or API keys
  • Enable Banking OAuth tokens or credentials
  • SimpleFIN setup tokens or access URLs
  • SMTP passwords or email content
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • IP addresses or device identifiers

Third-party services

Stripe — processes payments. When you purchase Klartion, Stripe collects your email and payment information. Their privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

Enable Banking (EU bank connections) — provides regulated open banking API access to your bank on your behalf. Their privacy policy governs how they handle your banking authorisation. Klartion stores the resulting access tokens locally on your machine only.

SimpleFIN Bridge (US/Canadian bank connections, optional) — if you use SimpleFIN, you sign up and pay them directly at bridge.simplefin.org. Their privacy policy governs how they handle your bank credentials. Klartion stores the resulting access URL (encrypted) locally on your machine only.

Notion — Klartion writes transaction data to a Notion database you own. Notion's privacy policy governs how they handle that data.

How we use your data

Your email address is used solely to:

  • Deliver your licence key after purchase
  • Respond to support requests you initiate
  • Send important notices about your licence (e.g. expiry)

We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data retention

We retain your email address and licence activation records for as long as your licence is active or until you request deletion. You can request deletion at any time by emailing support@david-alves.com. Stripe's retention policies apply to payment records.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email support@david-alves.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Cookies

Klartion's web interface uses a single session cookie to maintain your login state. This cookie is stored locally on your machine and is not used for tracking or analytics.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Your continued use of Klartion after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, email support@david-alves.com. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
